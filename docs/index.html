02 / Explicit permission · speech filter

One source. Chosen by you.
Its speech, filtered.

Request a tab through your browser’s picker. With speech filtering on, Nurture captures that tab’s audio, transcribes it locally with Whisper tiny.en, and mutes or beeps every word your rules and enabled word lists match. Audio and video are delayed a few seconds so words are silenced before you hear them; captions show replacements with an asterisk. Pixels and on-screen text are not filtered. Do not share sensitive content.

Speech model not loaded.

Nothing is shared. No recording or uploads.

The picker may offer screens as well as tabs; choose carefully. You cannot access another person’s browser without their separate sharing action and permission. Switching sources requires stopping and choosing again. In Chrome, choose a tab and keep “Also share tab audio” on; window and screen captures usually carry no audio on macOS. The original tab is silenced locally while shared so only the filtered audio plays. Speech recognition misses and mishears words, especially in music, crosstalk and accents—treat this as a preference tool, not a guarantee.

03 / Actual tab text

Change text where you read it.

The companion Chromium extension applies the same rules to ordinary page text after you explicitly enable it on the current tab. It includes a stop-and-restore control and a small asterisk next to replacements.

Extension source & installation →
Prototype boundaries

No blanket all-site permission, background tab surveillance or content uploads. Password fields, forms, editable regions, scripts and embedded frames are excluded. Canvas, images, video, audio and shadow DOM are not filtered by the extension; use section 02 for a shared tab’s speech. Some dynamic sites may be incompatible; this is not a security boundary.

04 / Trained English models

Compact models.
Measured, not magical.

Two balanced-context research models: a 64 KiB character-context classifier and quantized BERT Mini. Training uses public CC0 Toxic Spans and human-rated negative Civil Comments—not your tabs. Additional negative examples reduce false alarms, but quoted words remain difficult. These models do not meet the automatic safety-filter release gate; review highlights before relying on them.

No model is loaded. Highlighted words are review suggestions; only your approved word rules can supply replacements.

Real training

Deduplicated train, validation and held-out test sets. Checkpoints and thresholds selected on validation only. Provenance, seeds and training logs are published.

Efficient inference

INT8 weights, overlapping 128-token chunks, batched inference and a single-threaded WASM worker. No main-thread inference, CDN or required GPU.

Honest evaluation

Character-span F1, precision, recall, latency distributions, throughput and quantization comparisons. Empty annotated spans do not prove benign text. Native and browser results stay separate.

Measured in the browser

2,000 span-test comments (previously inspected) and 5,000 supplemental human-rated negative controls. F1 is not classification accuracy.
